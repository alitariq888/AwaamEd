Whole-route cost adds each year's cost grown by inflation. Exam and registration fees are one-off and not inflated. Transport = distance × 2 trips × school days × the per-km rate of your mode (school van is a flat monthly fee). Tuition scholarships on O-Level or Matric grades can cut A-Level and FSc school fees; lower the fee line to reflect an offer.
