# # AwaamEd

**What will school really cost, from now until university?**

🔗 Live app: https://alitariq888.github.io/AwaamEd/

AwaamEd is an open-access platform that helps Pakistani families compare the *real*, long-term cost of secondary-education pathways — not just the tuition sticker price. Most families compare monthly fees. AwaamEd compares the whole route: tuition, exam fees, transport, private tutoring, and the years it takes, side by side, so a family can see what O/A-Levels, Matric/FSc, or the GED actually cost them from today until university.

## Why this exists

In Pakistan, the choice between O/A-Levels, Matric/FSc, and alternative routes like the GED is usually made on reputation and rumour, not on real numbers. Hidden costs — private tuition, transport, exam-board fees that move with the dollar — are rarely counted until a family is already committed. AwaamEd makes those costs visible and comparable, upfront.

## What it does

- **Cost calculator** — compares four real pathways (O/A-Levels at school, O-Levels + private-candidate A-Levels, Matric/FSc, and the GED) across every cost category, in PKR, per month, per year, or for the whole route.
- **Editable, sourced data** — every fee is tagged *Collected* (from a real fee card, exam-board schedule, or interview) or *Estimate* (to be verified), and any family can override a number with their own.
- **Distance-based transport** — cost is calculated from actual distance and mode of travel (van, ride-hailing, own vehicle, walking), not a flat guess.
- **Family budget planner** — turns the cost comparison into a monthly budget: what share of household income each route takes, and how much to save per month starting today.

## Data

Phase 1 data is hyper-localised to Lahore, gathered directly from schools, exam boards, and family interviews, starting with the founder's own household. Every figure is dated and sourced. The dataset will expand city by city and is intended to be republished annually as an AwaamEd Education Inflation Report.

## Status

Phase 1 MVP — in active development, August 2026. Single-page app, no backend, no user data collected or stored.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step — the whole app is one file, hosted on GitHub Pages.
