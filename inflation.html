What this means for education specifically

April 2024The Education CPI sub-index specifically ran at 15.4% year-on-year — above the headline rate that month.
July 2026Education inflation was reported at 9.0% year-on-year, up from 8.3% the month before.

These are the two clearest education-specific readings found in official-data aggregators — not a full year-by-year series. Pakistan's Consumer Price Index does publish an Education sub-index every month, but a clean, continuous multi-year Education-only series wasn't something we could verify well enough to chart yet. That gap is exactly what AwaamEd's own dataset is meant to fill over time.

Why fees are legally capped — and why the cap doesn't always hold

Private school fees in Punjab aren't unregulated. Under the Punjab Private Educational Institutions (Promotion and Regulation) Ordinance, 1984 (as amended), a school charging PKR 4,000/month or more may raise its fee by at most 5% a year, or up to 8% with the regulator's prior approval — and the ordinance explicitly names O-Level, A-Level, and GCSE-preparing institutions as covered. A separate federal regulator, PEIRA, applies the same 5%/8% structure to schools in Islamabad.

Where it breaks down: Pakistan's National Assembly Standing Committee on Federal Education has publicly criticised PEIRA for weak enforcement, saying elite schools continue to impose increases well beyond the legal ceiling with little consequence. In practice, this is consistent with what this site's own founder-collected data shows — a single school's tuition range spanning far more than 8% year to year is not unusual.
Sources: Punjab Private Educational Institutions (Promotion and Regulation) Ordinance 1984, hed.punjab.gov.pk · Dawn, reporting on PEIRA guidelines and National Assembly committee proceedings

The other driver: a weaker rupee

Cambridge, Edexcel, and GED exam fees are set in US dollars, then billed in PKR — so even a frozen dollar fee gets more expensive in rupee terms whenever the exchange rate moves. It has moved a great deal:

2021Around PKR 150–170 per USD for most of the year
Jan 2023PKR 226.5 per USD
Sep 2023PKR 307.75 per USD — the all-time high
Aug 2026PKR ~278 per USD — well off the peak, still far above 2021

A GED test fee fixed at USD 90 cost roughly PKR 13,500–15,300 at 2021 rates. At the September 2023 peak it briefly cost over PKR 27,700 — more than double — before settling back to around PKR 25,000 today. The dollar amount never changed; the rupee did.

Sources: exchange-rates.org and Trading Economics historical USD/PKR data

Why this isn't the whole picture yet

  • The chart above is national CPI — a broad basket of goods across all of Pakistan. It is not the same as tracking actual tuition receipts from Lahore schools year over year.
  • Private-school and tutoring fees are widely reported to move differently from the headline rate, sometimes faster, since school committees often set fees annually rather than adjusting continuously — headline CPI is a reasonable proxy, not a substitute for real fee data.
  • AwaamEd's own dataset only has one year of collected fees so far (Lahore, 2026). A genuine local tuition-inflation line — the kind this page is ultimately meant to show — needs at least two to three years of Collected figures for the same schools, which is what the annual AwaamEd Education Inflation Report is building toward.
