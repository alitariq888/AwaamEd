Scholarships & Financial Aid

Funding that can lower the numbers on the calculator

Real government and donor-funded programmes for Pakistani students. Every entry is sourced; check the official portal before relying on any date or amount.

Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship Program

Federal · need & merit-based · public universities only
Undergraduate onlyFull tuition + stipendPublic sector universities only

Who it's for

Pakistani students newly admitted on merit to an HEC-recognised public-sector university, from households below a family-income threshold set by HEC (widely cited around PKR 45,000/month, but confirm the current figure on the portal). Run by the Higher Education Commission under the Prime Minister's Ehsaas programme.

What it covers

Full tuition fees for the duration of the degree, plus a monthly stipend, as long as the student maintains satisfactory academic standing (commonly cited as CGPA 3.0 or above, with no failed courses).

How to apply

Applications go through the university's Student Financial Aid Office alongside admission, not directly to HEC. Required documents include CNIC (or B-Form if under 18), income proof, and recent utility bills. Application windows open periodically — the portal is closed outside those windows, so timing matters.

Worth knowing: this scheme is undergraduate-only and public-university-only. Students on a private-university track, or already past their first year, don't qualify for a fresh award.
Source: Higher Education Commission of Pakistan, hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/Ehsaas

PEEF — Punjab Educational Endowment Fund

Provincial · covers Matric and Intermediate, not just university
Matric & Intermediate tooPunjab domicileMostly auto-nominated

Who it's for

The one most directly relevant to families using this calculator: PEEF funds Matric and Intermediate (FSc/FA) students, not just university. Eligibility: passed Matric or Intermediate in the annual exam through a Punjab or Federal board, scored at least 60%, family income at or below PKR 60,000/month, and enrolled full-time in the same year as passing.

What it covers

A scholarship stipend at the relevant level (Matric, Intermediate, Bachelor's, or Master's). Since 2009, PEEF has awarded over 475,000 scholarships and disbursed more than PKR 30 billion.

How to apply

Unusually, most PEEF awards under the "Outreach" category are not applied for — PEEF pulls top scorers directly from board results and contacts them. Special Quota (orphans, disabled students, minorities) and Master's-level awards do require an application. Check peef.org.pk for which category applies.

Worth knowing: because most awards are auto-nominated from board result data, there's no form to fill in to be considered for Outreach scholarships — scoring 60%+ in a Punjab or Federal board exam and meeting the income threshold is what puts a student in the pool.
Source: Punjab Educational Endowment Fund, peef.org.pk/outreach-scholarships

SEEF — Sindh Education Endowment Fund

Provincial · university level · Sindh domicile
University levelSindh domicileAnnual re-application

Who it's for

Students with Sindh domicile, enrolled at a SEEF-panel public or private university in fields including Engineering, IT, Medicine (MBBS/BDS/Pharmacy), Business, Law, or Social Sciences, with a GPA of 2.5/60% or above and family income within the fund's threshold.

What it covers

Tuition fees for one academic year at a time — renewal means reapplying, since the award is not automatically continued into the next year.

How to apply

Applications open annually (recent cycles have closed 31 August) via seef.sindh.gov.pk, with a hard copy submitted through the student's university. Final-year, overseas, and fully self-financed students are not eligible.

Source: Government of Sindh, College Education Department, seef.sindh.gov.pk

Pak-USAID Merit & Needs-Based Scholarship

Federal · specific fields · partner universities only
Tuition + housing + stipend~30 partner universitiesField-restricted

Who it's for

Academically strong, financially disadvantaged students admitted to one of roughly 30 partner universities, in Agriculture, Business, Engineering, Medical, or Social Sciences. Jointly run by HEC and USAID since 2004.

What it covers

Full tuition, student housing, textbooks, and a food stipend — one of the more comprehensive packages on this list, not tuition-only.

How to apply

Applications are handled by the financial aid office of each partner university, not centrally through HEC or USAID — check whether your target university is on the current partner list before applying elsewhere.

Source: Higher Education Commission of Pakistan, hec.gov.pk · USAID Pakistan programme archive

GED fee waivers

A common question — and an honest answer
Not available in Pakistan

What families often assume

GED Testing Service does run a fee-waiver programme — up to four waivers of USD 30 each toward test fees, for candidates who show financial hardship and pass a GED Ready practice test.

The honest answer: this waiver programme, along with state-funded free-testing schemes (Texas, New York, and others), is specific to test-takers based in the United States. It is not available to private candidates testing in Pakistan. Search results advertising "GED scholarships" for Pakistani students are, almost without exception, US domestic programmes that don't apply here — worth knowing before spending time on an application that was never open to you.

What actually helps instead

For the GED route, the realistic cost-reduction options are self-study instead of a prep centre (see the Pathway Library), and the general scholarships above if the plan afterward is a Pakistani public university, since GED holders with IBCC equivalence can apply to the same undergraduate scholarships as Matric/FSc or O/A-Level graduates.

Source: GED Testing Service fee-waiver guidance (US state programmes)

This list currently focuses on Punjab and Sindh, plus federal programmes — provincial funds for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, and private/NGO scholarships, are the next additions. Amounts, income thresholds, and application windows change yearly: always confirm on the official portal linked in each source before applying.
