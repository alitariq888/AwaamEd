The Founder

Why I'm building this.

Between O-Levels, a period of financial uncertainty, and eventually the GED, I moved through more of Pakistan's education systems than most families ever compare directly — and kept asking the same question: how much of a student's path comes down to ability, and how much to a family's ability to pay.

Private schooling, tutoring, and alternative credentials have each grown into separate, largely hidden cost layers on top of tuition. AwaamEd exists to make that full cost visible and comparable, so a family's choice can be driven by fit — not by which costs happen to show up first.

— Ali Tariq, founder
